Logo options for Alpine Labs and Ski Area Design Challenge

Each option is a matching pair: the studio's mark and the game's mark grown from the same idea. A is developed and is my recommendation; B and C are rough sketches of the two other ideas that survived, shown so you can say whether either is worth developing. Each is shown on sign white and graphite, from large down to the 24 px taskbar and 16 px title-bar sizes (drawn into real pixels, then enlarged), on a dark taskbar, and in place on the loading box and the title signpost.

How these were made. Three designers proposed 15 themes from different angles; a critic who drew none of them cut them to four. After each drawing, two reviewers who hadn't seen it judged the rendered board, one for what each mark literally reads as at every size, one for clichés and look-alikes, and the marks were redrawn. This is the third drawing. One idea was dropped (below). Still drafts. Wordmarks use Overpass as it comes; the chosen pair gets a final pass on spacing and outlined type before it goes into the game. Your eye decides: reviewers like these can catch misreadings, not judge taste.
Dropped after review: Queue Line (the lift-line rope maze). Both reviewers found the game mark reads as the numeral 2, so next to the title it says "Ski Area Design Challenge 2", a sequel; the studio mark read as an S or the hamburger menu. If you want a fourth option, I can develop another theme.
The name: "Alpine" is a crowded mark (Renault's Alpine cars, Alpine Electronics), and a camera-gear company has traded as Alpine Labs. Worth a trademark search before the name goes public. Survey orange stays out of every mark: the game keeps it for planned work.
