# 4.14 ponds, dams, water: owner findings

The owner's findings from demoing PR A (demo 117: ponds, dams and lakes as sources on the ground) on the Doublehead and
Kearsarge maps, 2026-11-16. Each finding is recorded as raised. The owner then approved plan v2 (2026-11-17), which answers
them and keeps the game true to the MapLibre version while building on what Unity already has. The fixes are below the
findings table.

| # | Finding | Where | Fix (plan v2) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | In the original MapLibre game, lakes, creeks and streams could be selected and named, and their properties seen. The Unity version can't do that. | Demo 117, any map | Click (no tool armed) opens a card for a lake, stream, pond or dam: name (OpenStreetMap's or yours, renamable), area, elevation, depth, volume, width, flow, source switch; named water on the Labels layer; hover outline (the name in the hover readout is still to come). OpenStreetMap names come in `water.json`; older areas fetch them the first time they open online | Built, awaiting demo |
| F2 | Dams seem to work fine on the Kearsarge and Doublehead maps. | Demo 117, Kearsarge and Doublehead | None needed; good dam sites there become the real-ground test fixtures | Recorded |
| F3 | While building a dam, the preview should be better. | Demo 117, Dam tool | The streams in blue and the full-pool contour while picking the banks; in review, the graded embankment, the reservoir as water at full pool with its outline, the crest and the spillway; a refusal marks where the problem is. Built: the streams in blue, the full pool in the first bank's hint, the full-pool contour; in review the graded ground, the water and its shore, the spillway dot and a red dot for a PD-13 bed, the line red when refused (the pond review shows its ground and water too). Not yet: the floating reservoir tag and the water-side arrow | Built, awaiting demo |
| F4 | The scripted dam tests were ridiculous (a dam built on a cliff's edge). Put guardrails in the game for this reason. | The `-watershots` capture's dam on a cliff edge at Jackson Hole | PD-10 cliff edge (refused), PD-11 water held vs earth moved, PD-12 the first bank beside a stream, PD-13 a minimum dam height; tests on real ground: `TestData/dams`, six dams found along the OSM streams of Doublehead and Kearsarge (four good, one PD-11, one PD-13; `DamFixtureTests`). No real cliff site turned up (the New England valleys are gentle, and the Jackson Hole core's streams cross the flat valley floor), so PD-10 keeps its synthetic cliff test | Built, awaiting demo |
| F5 | A player should be able to build empty ponds, or ponds that don't fill up naturally, then build a pump house to fill them from another source. | Demo 117, Pond tool | Pond options Fills (Naturally / Pumped only) and Starts (Full / Empty), Starts for dams too; the simulation (PR B) moves water between sources for 4.15's pump house | Options built and saved (PR A); PR B: empty starts, pumped-only ponds take no runoff, `TransferWater` ready for 4.15 |
