Brand boards

Basic branding for each art direction. A, B and C use the working title Mountain Planner; D keeps the MapLibre edition's own title, Ski Area Design Challenge. Each board has the logo, palette and typefaces, then the brand in use: an app icon, a title screen and a store banner over our Unity mountain. It ends with one signature item and a sample of how the game talks to you. Logos and names are first sketches, not final artwork.
