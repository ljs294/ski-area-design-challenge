Your layout, playable

The layout from your sketch as a clickable mockup, in the Trailhead look over our Unity mountain. The status bar runs the full width of the bottom, docked to the edge or floating; the clock and weather run, the tools draw on the mountain, and building costs money. Nothing is saved when you reload.

  • Build a lift: Toolbox → Lifts → New, click the base station, then the top station, choose a model, then Break ground. Save plan keeps it as an orange plan instead.
  • Draw a trail: Toolbox → Trails → New, click down the mountain, then double-click or press Enter to finish. Snowmaking and Infrastructure place guns, water lines, pump houses, lodges, roads and parking the same way.
  • The menu (top right) has quick switches for the theme (dark, light, or auto by the sun), the status bar (docked or floating) and units, and opens Settings: interface, units and time, gameplay, graphics, audio and controls. Interface scale, the clock, the balance format, autosave, pause while building, confirm before removing, tooltips, notifications and zoom all work; graphics and audio are stored only.
  • Also live: play/pause and four speeds, save (white when there are changes), Analysis with six tabs, the sketch pencil and map layers. Scroll to zoom. The bar shows the resort's name with whether the lifts are running (try the three styles above the frame; click the name to rename it), the weather with the next 24 hours' snowfall, and the elevation under the pointer. Keys: T toolbox, Tab analysis, U units, Space pause, 1–4 speed, + − zoom, Home reset view, the tool letters, Esc to back out, Ctrl+S to save.
