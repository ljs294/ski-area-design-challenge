Your layout, playable

The layout from your sketch as a clickable mockup, in the Trailhead look over our Unity mountain. The status bar docks into the corner as you drew it, or floats; the clock and weather run, the tools draw on the mountain, and building costs money. Nothing is saved when you reload.

  • Build a lift: Toolbox → Lifts → New, click the base station, then the top station, choose a model, then Break ground. Save plan keeps it as an orange plan instead.
  • Draw a trail: Toolbox → Trails → New, click down the mountain, then double-click or press Enter to finish. Snowmaking and Infrastructure place guns, water lines, pump houses, lodges, roads and parking the same way.
  • Also live: the dock button at the right end of the bar (docked or floating), play/pause and four speeds, save (white when there are changes), Analysis, the sketch pencil, map layers, the menu, zoom and the compass. The bar shows the elevation under the pointer. Keys: T toolbox, A analysis, Space pause, 1–4 speed, the tool letters, Esc to back out, Ctrl+S to save.

An interactive style mockup, not the game: the mountain is a capture of our Unity build with the sky composited behind it. The numbers, costs, weather and the elevation readout are placeholders (the opening values in the bar come from your sketch).
