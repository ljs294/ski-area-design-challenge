Your layout, playable

The layout from your sketch as a clickable mockup, in the Trailhead look over our Unity mountain. The clock runs, the tools draw on the mountain, and building costs money. Nothing is saved when you reload.

  • Build a lift: Toolbox → Lifts → New Lift, click the base station, then the top station, choose a model, then Break ground. Save plan keeps it as an orange plan instead.
  • Draw a trail: Toolbox → Trails → New Trail, click down the mountain, then double-click or press Enter to finish. Snowmaking and Infrastructure tools place guns, pipes, lodges, roads and parking the same way.
  • Also live: play/pause and speed, save, Analysis, the sketch pencil (draw on the map), map layers, the menu, zoom and the compass. Keys: T toolbox, A analysis, Space pause, 1–3 speed, the letter on each tool, Esc to back out, Ctrl+S to save.

An interactive style mockup, not the game: the mountain is a capture of our Unity build with the sky composited behind it, and the numbers, costs and names are placeholders (the opening values in the bar come from your sketch).
