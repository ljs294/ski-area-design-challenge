Your layout, built out

The layout from your sketch, in the Trailhead look, over the bluebird capture of our Unity mountain. The status bar is compact at the bottom left, the map controls sit at the top right, and the camera controls at the bottom right. The Toolbox opens a tabbed tray of tools, and each tool opens its own floating build panel.

1 · General view nothing open

  • Status bar, left to right: Toolbox and Analysis; play, the day and the clock with the weather; speed; cash; today's earnings, red when negative; lifts operating; skiers on the mountain; and save, which turns amber with a dot when there are unsaved changes.
  • Top right: sketch (blueprint), map layers and menu, as one group. Bottom right: compass (north up), 3D, zoom in and zoom out.

2 · Toolbox open tabs, then tools with symbols

  • The Toolbox button turns white while its tray is open. The tray sits just above the bar and has four tabs: Lifts, Trails, Snowmaking and Infrastructure.
  • Every tool is a symbol with its name: New, Edit and Remove for lifts. The shortcut key sits in the corner of each tile.

3 · New Lift the tool's floating build panel

  • Choosing New Lift opens a floating panel that you can drag anywhere. It holds the lift type and model, the numbers as you draw, the profile, a cost breakdown and Break ground.
  • On the map, the plan is surveyor's orange: a dashed line with a dimension, drawn from base to top station.

A style frame, not a working UI. The mountain is a capture of our Unity build, with the sky and clouds composited behind it. The lifts, runs, names and numbers are placeholders; the ones in the bar come from your sketch.
