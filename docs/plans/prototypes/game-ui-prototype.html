  1. Plan a lift: pick Lifts in the bar, choose a lift, then click a bottom terminal and a top terminal. Drag a blue handle to reshape a blueprint.
  2. Paint a run: pick Trails (the slope-angle view opens by itself), click down the hill, and press Enter or double-click to finish.
  3. Build: open the Cost tab and press Build blueprints. Nothing is paid until then.
  4. Run a winter: press play. The season opens on December 1; try the Lift lines and Skier traffic views, and click a lift or run.

Space play or pause · Enter finish a run · Backspace remove the last point · Esc cancel or close
