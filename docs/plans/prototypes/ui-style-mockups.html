Resort HUD Directions

One resort, one moment, two styles. It's midwinter at a made-up mountain; three lifts and five runs are open, and you're planning a lift to the summit. Each frame shows the same things: the build tool, one selected lift, a data view and the status bar. These are still mockups, not playable.

Leaning on Cities: Skylines II

  • Dark, translucent panels with small uppercase headings; the mountain shows through.
  • A two-tier command bar across the bottom: resort tier and needs, then colourful category icons; below, time, weather, name, skiers, money and happiness.
  • An asset drawer of lift cards above the bar, with a tool-options panel beside it. You place directly, with length, rise, angle and cost drawn live in the world.
  • Info views from the top-left grid: here Skier traffic, which pales the mountain so busy runs glow. Notification icons float over trouble spots.

Leaning on Subway Builder

  • Flat, minimal panels in light or dark, with bold short labels and segmented choices.
  • Round tool buttons at the bottom left, and a slim status strip: time, speed, money with today's change, skiers.
  • One Construction window with Edit and Cost. You draw blueprints first; cost chips sit on the map; Build blueprints pays for them all.
  • A schematic map where lifts read like transit lines, with lift lines as sized bubbles. A Map Layers checklist sits top-right, a legend bottom-right.
