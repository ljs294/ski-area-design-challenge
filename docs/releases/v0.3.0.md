**Early test build.** Phase 3 of the rebuild: you can now draw a ski area on a real mountain.

**Download:** under **Assets**, click `SkiAreaDesignChallenge-0.3.0-win64.zip` (about 360 MB). Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit, with a DirectX 11 or 12 graphics card.

**The game isn't signed yet.** Before unzipping, right-click the zip › **Properties** › tick **Unblock** › **OK**. If
Windows still says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New in 0.3

- **Lifts:** fixed-grip and detachable chairlifts. Click the bottom, then the top. Towers are placed
  automatically along the rope and its sag, or you move them by hand.
- **Trails:** paint a run with a brush. Grading is optional: it benches the trail into the slope with cut and fill,
  and too-steep stretches stay natural. Trails are rated by their steepness.
- **Terrain brushes:** raise, lower, flatten, smooth and slope the ground. Trees and towers follow the brush, and
  you can clear trees. Confirm or revert each session.
- **The network:** lifts and trails join into one network, with names on the lines and an overview under Analysis.
- **Saves:** named saves per area (Ctrl+S) plus an autosave every 10 minutes. Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
- **Send feedback:** in the menu. It saves a report (screenshot, logs, your save, your computer's details) and opens
  a GitHub issue to drop it into. If the game closed by itself, the next start offers a report.

## Known limits

- Grading a long run takes about a second to preview.
- On a very large resort, the trail look covers about 2 km around the view.
- Minimum hardware hasn't been measured yet. If it runs slowly, lower Settings › Graphics › Quality preset.

Please report anything confusing, slow or broken with **Send feedback**, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/ljs294/ski-area-design-challenge/issues).
