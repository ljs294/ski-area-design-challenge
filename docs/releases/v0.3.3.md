**Early test build.** The resort comes alive: the clock runs, guests arrive, ski and ride your lifts, lifts run
and hold in the wind, and every area plays its own weather. And a new installer keeps the game up to date.

**Download:** under **Assets**, click `SkiAreaDesignChallenge-Setup.exe` (under 1 MB) and run it. Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit, with a DirectX 11 or 12 graphics card.

**The game isn't signed yet**, so Windows says "Windows protected your PC" once: click **More info**, then **Run
anyway**. Click **Install**: it downloads the game (about 400 MB) into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SkiAreaDesignChallenge`
(no administrator needed, and no "Unblock" step), adds a Start-menu entry and, if you like, a desktop shortcut, and
ends on **Launch Ski Area Design Challenge v0.3.3**.

**Coming from 0.3.2?** It can't update itself, so run the Setup once. Your areas and saves are kept outside the game
folder and carry over; delete the old unzipped folder whenever you like.

**Opening your mountain:** in this test build the season starts from the developer panel. Build a lift and a trail
down from it, press **F1** and click **Start the season**. Until then the lifts stay parked and nobody comes.

## New in 0.3.3

- **Installer and updates:** from now on, every start checks GitHub for a new version and asks before updating.
  Update downloads it, checks it, and ends on the Launch screen. The version before stays installed, and **Other
  versions** (or holding Shift as the game starts) plays it or an older release. The check sends nothing about you or
  your computer; **Settings › Data › Check for updates** turns it off, and without an internet connection the game
  starts as usual. To uninstall: Start menu › Ski Area Design Challenge › **Uninstall Ski Area Design Challenge**.
- **Time passes:** the clock runs a ski year from October 1, with a 42-day season from December to April. A day on
  the hill takes about 12 minutes at 1×. Space pauses, and 1 to 4 pick 1×, 2×, 4× or 8×. The bar shows the day and
  date, the sun follows the clock, and saves keep the day and the time.
- **Guests:** up to 3,000 skiers and snowboarders arrive, pick the runs and lifts that suit their ability, wait in
  line, ride, ski and go home. How their day went sizes tomorrow's crowd. Near the camera each ability skis its own
  way (a wedge, skidded turns, short turns or carving), they stand in lines as wide as the chair and ride chairs,
  T-bars and carpets; far off they're coloured dots. Click one for its card, with Follow, Next skier and Next in this
  line.
- **Lifts run:** lines and waits, opening hours (the resort's or the lift's own), Daily or Peak days only, and wind
  holds: tows and carpets never hold, fixed grips hold later than detachables. The lift card shows Status, Line, Wind
  hold, Today, Runs and Hours, and the Lifts list shows each wait.
- **Ride a lift:** the video-camera button on a lift's card puts you on the chair, under the T-bar or on the carpet,
  looking around with the mouse. Space pauses, H hides the bar, Esc takes you back, and you get off at the top.
- **Lift plates:** give a lift a 1 to 3 character plate (the "#" on its card, or Shift+F2); it leads the Lifts list and
  the map label.
- **Traffic layer** (Shift+5, or Layers › Traffic): runs coloured by how crowded they are, lifts by how loaded, each
  line's length and wait, and dots by Ability, State or Happiness.
- **Weather:** every area plays weather fitted to its own records, or a typical western or eastern mountain's until it
  has its own. New downloads have a Weather step; for an older area, use **Get weather** in Manage Areas (it's also
  tried by itself when the area opens online). The line under the clock shows base and summit temperatures and the
  next 24 hours' snow; notices warn of storms, rain, thaws, cold snaps and high wind; a lift card says "Holds likely"
  before a windy day. A powder morning brings more skiers, and rain during the ski day keeps them home. Weather
  doesn't change the snow on the map or show in the sky yet.
- **Posted trail signs:** choose a trail's sign (● ■ ◆ ◆◆) in New trail's Posted row before Break ground, and change it
  in Edit trail. Everything shows your sign; guests notice a run posted too easy.
- **Analysis lists:** Trails shows Popularity and Status, Lifts shows Popularity, and a click on a column title sorts
  or filters.
- **Prices for everything:** surface lifts and trails have a Cost row, and lift and trail prices include Grading (cut
  and fill) and Clearing (only trees newly cut).
- **Quicker editing:** with graded runs built, grading a run, removing one or adding a lift is about twice as quick.

## Fixed in 0.3.3

- The game now runs on DirectX 11, which ends the quit crash some PCs still had in 0.3.2, and it's a little faster.
- Resorts with many lifts run smoothly again: the turning terminal wheels cost too much in 0.3.2.
- On big areas every trail shows its groomed look, not only those within about 2 km of the middle.
- A long Analysis list scrolls instead of running off the screen at 150% UI scale.
- The Guests tab in Analysis shows its own note.

## Saves

Your downloaded areas and saves carry over. Saves from 0.3.2 open and are updated when you save them again: trails keep
their colours, lifts start without plates, and the clock starts at October 1. Saves made in 0.3.3, and areas that get
their weather, don't open in 0.3.2, so keep that in mind before playing 0.3.2 from **Other versions**.

Known: the ride button's tooltip names the R key, but there's no ride key (R tilts the camera).
