**Early test build.** More lifts, a choice of drive end, lift prices, and downloads that tell you when a server is
holding them up.

**Download:** under **Assets**, click `SkiAreaDesignChallenge-0.3.2-win64.zip` (about 400 MB). Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit, with a DirectX 11 or 12 graphics card.

**The game isn't signed yet.** Before unzipping, right-click the zip › **Properties** › tick **Unblock** › **OK**. If
Windows still says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New in 0.3.2

- **More lifts:** fixed-grip doubles and triples from Sessellift and Monta, the Sessellift D4 detachable quad, and
  the Sessellift Stern quad, triple and double.
- **Drive at either end:** on every fixed-grip chairlift, choose whether the drive sits at the top or the bottom
  (the Drive row in New lift and Edit lift). The top terminal's sheave row is picked for the rope's angle.
- **Lift prices:** New lift and Edit lift show what a chairlift costs. Hover the Cost row to see the parts.
- **Building lifts is quicker:** towers are numbered on the profile and over the lift; drag a tower on the profile
  to move it or change its height; Auto-place fills in the rest. Automatic lines use fewer, taller towers.
- **Lift lines are cleared** through the forest, and snow lies in the clearings.
- **Downloads held up by a server** now say which one after 2 minutes, with Try now, Pause for later and Cancel,
  and a choice to skip water and roads.
- **Get water and roads** in Manage Areas, for an area that was downloaded without them.

## Fixed in 0.3.2

- The game no longer crashes as it closes on some DirectX 12 graphics cards.

Your downloaded areas and saves carry over: saves from 0.3.1 open and are updated when you save them again.

Please report anything confusing, slow or broken with **Send feedback**, or
