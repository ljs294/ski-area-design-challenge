**Early test build.** The mountain gets real snow and weather: storms you can see, snow that falls, settles, drifts
and melts cell by cell, trails that open by their snow, lakes that freeze, ponds and dams you build, the first
snowmaking pipes and guns, a gondola, and groomers you buy and crew.

**Download:** under **Assets**, click `SkiAreaDesignChallenge-Setup.exe` (under 1 MB) and run it. Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit, with a DirectX 11 or 12 graphics card.

**Already on 0.3.3?** Just start the game: it offers 0.3.4 and updates itself.

**The game isn't signed yet**, so a fresh install says "Windows protected your PC" once: click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**. Click **Install**: it downloads the game (about 400 MB) into
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SkiAreaDesignChallenge` (no administrator needed), adds a Start-menu entry and, if you like,
a desktop shortcut, and ends on **Launch Ski Area Design Challenge v0.3.4**.

**Opening your mountain:** in this test build the season still starts from the developer panel. Build a lift and a
trail down from it, press **F1** and click **Start the season**.

## New in 0.3.4

- **Weather you can see:** clouds and a cloud deck you can fly into and above, valley fog, sun rays, falling snow and
  rain, storm fronts that travel, cloud shadows on the ground, trees that lean in the wind and spindrift off the
  ridges. **Settings › Weather** turns the whole picture, or any part of it, off; the weather itself still plays.
- **Live snow:** the snow on the map now comes from the weather. Every 8 m of the mountain gets its own snow: it falls,
  settles, drifts off ridges, slides off steep rock, and melts faster on sunny south faces than in the shade. Deep
  snow sits on the ground as its own layer. **Shift+9** shows snow depth and **Shift+0** the snow conditions
  (powder, packed powder, groomed, hard packed, icy, corn and more), each with a legend and a readout under the cursor.
- **Trails open by their snow:** every hour each trail is rated **Covered**, **Marginal** or **Bare** by whether a
  skiable line runs all the way down it, and a trail without one closes. Skiers pack and wear the snow lane by lane;
  moguls grow on steep runs that aren't groomed, and ice forms where the snow is skied hard. Groomers smooth them
  overnight (see Grooming).
- **The trail card's Snow:** Conditions, Cover, Snow rating and a one-line surface ("Hard pack · bare patches ·
  moguls"), and a switch: **Auto** (open when the snow allows), **Open** or **Closed**. Analysis › Trails has a
  Conditions column, and Status says why a trail is closed ("Closed · no snow", "Closed by you").
- **Lakes freeze and thaw,** each in its own time by its depth: open water in the fall, then ice, then snow on the
  ice, and open again in the spring. With Snow depth on, hovering over water reads like "Pond · 4 in ice · 2 in snow".
- **Ponds and dams:** the Toolbox's **Snowmaking** tab builds ponds (click the shoreline) and dams across a stream,
  with a live preview of the water at full pool and guardrails against impossible dams. Lakes, streams, ponds and
  dams have cards (hover outlines one, a click opens it) with water level, volume and area; names show on the map
  and can be changed. Ponds and reservoirs fill from their streams and runoff through the season, and their cards
  show what comes in and goes out.
- **Snowmaking network:** pump houses, water lines, inline boosters, hydrants and snow guns, with live pressure and
  flow readouts, **Check network** (with Show me and fixes), the Snowmaking map layer and the Pipe pressure info
  layer. In this build the guns don't make snow on the trails yet; that comes next.
- **Gondola:** the Sessellift G8, an eight-seat cabin gondola. Guests ride gondolas up and down all day, and a chair
  that carries skiers down brings them back from its top so nobody is stranded. Ride it from its card to sit at a
  window seat.

- **Grooming:** Analysis has a **Grooming** tab. Buy groomers under **Equipment**; each trail chooses whether and
  when it's groomed, and the tab shows tonight's plan straight away, even during the day: how many hours of work it
  is, how much fits your crews, and what it costs. Equipment lists each groomer's hours on the Swing and Night
  shifts (8 hours each, from closing to opening), with one line to set crews or go back to Auto. Click a groomer on
  the map to see its path tonight, and watch the groomers drive along the trails between runs. Last night shows what
  was actually worked. **A new resort starts with no groomers**, so buy one before the season; saves from 0.3.3 are
  given a fleet when they open.

## Fixed in 0.3.4

- A save with lifts or trails kept its date, snow and guests when you opened it with **Continue** or **Load**. In
  0.3.3 such a save started its clock over ("The save's clock couldn't be read").
- A save with a dam opens with its clock.

## Saves

Your downloaded areas and saves carry over. Saves from 0.3.3 open and are updated when you save them again. Saves
made in 0.3.4 don't open in 0.3.3, so keep that in mind before playing 0.3.3 from **Other versions**.

Known:
- Snow guns are built and checked, but don't make snow yet.
- The season still starts from the F1 developer panel.
