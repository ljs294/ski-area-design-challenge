**Early test build.** A fix-up of 0.3.0: two fixes from the bug bash.

**Download:** under **Assets**, click `SkiAreaDesignChallenge-0.3.1-win64.zip` (about 360 MB). Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit, with a DirectX 11 or 12 graphics card.

**The game isn't signed yet.** Before unzipping, right-click the zip › **Properties** › tick **Unblock** › **OK**. If
Windows still says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Fixed in 0.3.1

- **Downloads no longer hang** at "waiting for OpenStreetMap (Overpass)". If the OpenStreetMap servers don't answer,
  the download skips map features after about a minute and finishes anyway: satellite land cover stands in for
  water and roads, and the area's quality card says so.
- **Bullwheels turn:** the wheels in the lift terminals now spin with the rope.

Everything else is as in [0.3.0](https://github.com/ljs294/ski-area-design-challenge/releases/tag/v0.3.0). Your
downloaded areas and saves carry over.

Please report anything confusing, slow or broken with **Send feedback**, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/ljs294/ski-area-design-challenge/issues).
