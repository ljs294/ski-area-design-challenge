The weekly report

How to read this. The weather is the engine exactly as the game would play it: one seeded season per button, from the site's frozen rows. The base depth is an illustration, not the game's snow (4.07 owns the snowpack): snow water accumulates and melts by the tests' reference model (snowpack temperature, a melt that follows the sun's season), and the depth settles toward a density of 0.30 cold, 0.38 near freezing and 0.45 wet. No grooming, wind loading, skier packing, snowmaking or terrain shade. Gusts are the engine's free air at the reference height during lift hours; exposed summits, lift top stations and wind holds come with the engine in the game (PR B). A game week is three face days (weekday, weekday, weekend), about 8.6 calendar days; the season is Day 1 (Dec 1) to Day 42 (Apr 1).
